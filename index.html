About Me

I am a Year 4 Computer Science undergraduate in the College of Computing and Data Science at Nanyang Technological University. I currently hold a CGPA of 4.78/5.00 and am on track for Highest Distinction. I also achieved a Graduate Record Examination score of 324/340, including 168 in Quantitative Reasoning.

My research experience spans visual analytics, human–AI collaboration, intelligent data systems, and database technologies. Through NTU's URECA programme under the supervision of Prof. Yong Wang, I contributed to DFCollection for scalable hierarchical data-fact exploration, FOX for visual exploration of data-fact outliers, and EvoMaestro for interpretable and steerable LLM-driven program evolution. These projects have led to publications accepted by Visual Informatics, IEEE VIS 2026, and ACM UIST 2026, respectively.

I am currently conducting collaborative research through Fudan University's Xiyuan Project under the supervision of Prof. Yong Wang and Prof. Ye Guangnan. My work includes FactorTriad, reinforcement-learning-based alpha factor generation, and UniStock, a unified framework for stock selection based on spatio-temporal dynamic modeling. Following my eight-week Summer Research Program at HKUST under the supervision of Prof. Raymond Chi-Wing Wong, I continue collaborating on natural-language querying over NoSQL databases, including schema compression, benchmark design, and query evaluation. From January to April 2027, I will also serve as a part-time Research Intern at A*STAR ARTC, focusing on image-data management, LLM-assisted querying, and research analytics.

Beyond my academic research, I led a four-person team to the semifinals of the SimplifyNext Agentic AI Hackathon, where we developed GradRoute Agent, a grounded agentic system for course-registration cases. My professional internship at Centricore gave me hands-on experience developing and maintaining production-level full-stack enterprise systems. I am particularly interested in pursuing PhD research in database systems and interdisciplinary directions at the intersections of databases, artificial intelligence, human-computer interaction, and visualization. I welcome opportunities to connect with prospective supervisors and research collaborators.

In preparation · First author · Fudan–NTU collaboration

Developing a visual analytics system for exploring alpha factor pools from structural, semantic, and behavioral perspectives through coordinated views, tailored similarity measures, and cross-space comparisons.

Reinforcement Learning for Interpretable Alpha Factor Generation (working title)

In preparation · Expected first author · Fudan–NTU collaboration

Developing structure-aware reinforcement learning for formula editing with dimension-consistent action masks, uncertainty-guided exploration, duplicate-aware learning, parallel evaluation, and interactive trajectory visualization.

UniStock: A Unified Framework for Stock Selection based on Spatio-Temporal Dynamic Modeling

In preparation · Expected co-author · Fudan–NTU collaboration

Developing a unified quantitative stock-selection framework that combines decoder-only or encoder-only time-series pretraining with dynamic hypergraph attention-based spatio-temporal fine-tuning. UniStock supports flexible input and prediction horizons, dynamically models relationships across stock pools, and is evaluated on long-term A-share and S&P 500 datasets.

Natural-Language Querying over NoSQL Databases (working title)

In preparation · Expected co-author · HKUST collaboration
